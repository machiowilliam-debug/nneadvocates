WHAT WE PRACTICE



Family Law

We handle family matters such as:divorce, legal separation, domestic partnerships, child custody/visitation, child support, allowance, domestic violence, property distribution.
We’re able to draw from our prior experience and wealth of knowledge to protect our clients’ best interests throughout the duration of the case.



Tax Law

We advises on various Kenyan tax-related issues including corporation tax, value added tax, withholding tax, compensating tax, excise duty, real estate tax, transfer tax, energy and infrastructure tax, stamp duty, VAT, transfer pricing, corporate restructuring, private equity, income and other employment taxes laws.



Arbitration  & Meditation 

Arbitration is a widely used means to end disputes. We labour to help our clients resolve disputes efficiently and effectively in a manner that promotes their organizational and personal objectives. Our lawyers have a wealth of experience in resolving disputes (both advocating for clients and sitting as arbitrators).



Banking & Finance Law  

The banking and financial industries are heavily regulated by law. Our lawyers advise on all aspects of banking, insurance and reinsurance law, whether corporate, regulatory or contentious. Our mix of specialist skills and experience has resulted in a industry focused team committed to the highest standards of client service.



Business Setup

Business setup is an exciting but without the right legal advice it can also be a legal minefield.

We provide advice on the regulatory environment for the various types of entities and the sectors within which they operate as well as deal with registration of companies, private public partnerships, business names and trusts.



Constitution & Administrative Law

The firm has developed excellent expertise in constitutional and administrative law matters particularly in relation to the 2010 Constitution. In this respect, the firm has advised on various provisions on the Constitution especially with regard to the Bill of Rights, Land, Citizenship, Devolved Government and Public Finance.



Corporate Law

Corporate law governs the functioning of companies; from how they are formed to the transactions they are permitted to engage in.

It applies to shareholders, directors, creditors and other stakeholders by regulating their rights and duties. It’s the body of law that regulates legal entities that exist to conduct business.



Real Estate Law

This laws deals with all stages of transfer of interests in land including; sales, leases, purchases, charges, tenancy agreements, commercial letting. Our lawyers provide advisory and transactional support in construction, real estate tax, real estate investment and development, real estate partnerships and fractional ownership.

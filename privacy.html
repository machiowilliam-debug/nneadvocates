Privacy notice

How we handle the personal information you share with us through this website.

Nyongesa Nafula & Co. Advocates respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how it is handled.

Information we collect

When you submit the consultation form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, the practice area you select and the description of your matter. We also receive the page from which the form was submitted.

How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry and, where you instruct us, to provide legal services. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

Confidentiality

Information submitted through the form is treated in confidence. Submitting an enquiry does not by itself create an advocate–client relationship; that relationship begins only when we confirm instructions in writing.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to the firm through Web3Forms, a third-party form service. The website is hosted and maintained using standard web infrastructure. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at info@nneadvocates.co.ke or write to us at AppleWood Adams, 7th Floor, Suite 705/706, Ngong Road, Nairobi.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be directed to info@nneadvocates.co.ke or +254 20 210 0150.

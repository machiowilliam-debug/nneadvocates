Who we are

Welcome to Nyongesa Nafula & Co. Advocates

Nyongesa Nafula & Co. Advocates is a premier law firm providing a comprehensive range of legal services and solutions for domestic and international clients. The firm has a reputation as one of the leading law firms in the East African region. International and cross-border work forms an important part of our business.

Our clients are private and public companies from Kenya and abroad, local and international banks, development finance agencies and non-government organisations in all sectors of the economy.

We advise on business law, corporate finance and capital markets, project finance, financial services, banking, power, telecommunications law, infrastructure, transport, manufacturing, tourism, agriculture and horticulture. We represent clients in litigation and provide dispute resolution advice in all of these areas.

Meet our team

How we work

A practice arranged around the client

Large transactions need more than one lawyer. They need a team that stays involved — and a partner who remains accountable.

01

Comprehensive advice

Our work spans business law, corporate finance, banking, infrastructure, real estate, tax and dispute resolution — so advice on one issue is informed by the others.

02

Partner-led teams

To meet the expectations of our clients we arrange our practice to provide teams of lawyers for large-scale transactions when needed. Our transaction teams are led by experienced partners who stay closely involved in all aspects of the matter.

03

Notary & commissioner services

In addition to being Kenyan advocates, the firm provides notary and commissioner services. A number of our lawyers are English trained, having gained experience as barristers or solicitors before coming to Kenya.

Our objective

High-quality advice, efficiently managed.

Our objective is to provide high quality legal and commercial advice through an efficiently managed professional practice that will facilitate our clients' business dealings in Kenya and elsewhere in the East African region.

With Kenya's commercial laws drawing on laws from Commonwealth jurisdictions and particularly the English system, there is a distinct advantage in having dual-qualified partners and associates in the practice.

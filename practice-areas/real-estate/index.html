Real Estate Law

A robust conveyancing department handling every stage of a property transaction.

Conveyancing and transfers

Our conveyancing department deals with all stages of the transfer of interests in land. We act for buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants and lenders.

  • Sales and purchases
  • Leases and tenancy agreements
  • Charges and security
  • Commercial letting
  • Transfers of interests in land

Advisory and transactional support

Our lawyers provide advisory and transactional support in construction, real estate tax, real estate investment and development, real estate partnerships and fractional ownership.

Financing and development

We guide clients through the processes involved in real estate financing and borrowing. We seek innovative solutions to project problems and steer projects to a successful conclusion.

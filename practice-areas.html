How we work

From first conversation to conclusion

Every matter is different, but the way we work is consistent: understand the facts, set a clear strategy and stay accountable for delivery.

01

Understand

We start by listening. A first conversation establishes the facts, the objective and the constraints before we advise on a course of action.

02

Advise

You receive a clear explanation of your options, the likely path ahead and the practical implications of each route.

03

Deliver

The advocate handling your matter stays with it — from first instruction through to completion or resolution.
